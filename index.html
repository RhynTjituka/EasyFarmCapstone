Why I'm building this

Farmers lose livestock, and when an animal goes missing it is hard to describe it well enough for anyone to recognise it. My family back home faces this, and it is why I am building EasyFarm.

The idea is simple: if every animal already has one clear photo and one ear tag in the app, a missing animal is never just a description. It is a picture.

How it works

  1. Capture. Photograph each animal once and record its ear tag. The app builds the herd database.
  2. Roll call. Scan the animals you see. The app compares them with the herd list and shows who is missing.
  3. Poster. For a missing animal, the app builds a MISSING poster around its photo, adds the farmer's details, and shares it to WhatsApp.

The photo check

A poster is only useful if the photo is. Like apps that verify an ID, EasyFarm will not accept a photo that is too dark, too bright, blurred, flat, or with the animal cut off. It tells the farmer what to fix and lets them retake it.

Status: the rules are written and tested. The camera screen that uses them is still to come.
