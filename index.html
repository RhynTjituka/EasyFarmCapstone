Why EasyFarm

Farmers lose livestock, and when an animal goes missing it is hard to describe it well enough for anyone to recognise it. My family back home faces this, and it is why I am building EasyFarm.

The idea: every animal gets one clear photo and one ear tag in the app. When one goes missing, the app already has what is needed to make a poster.

What EasyFarm does

  • Scan: the app opens on the camera, ready to read an ear tag or capture an animal.
  • Herd: a database of every animal, with its photo, ear tag, species and status.
  • Roll call: compare the animals you see with the herd list to find who is missing.
  • Missing poster: a MISSING notice built around the animal's photo plus the farmer's details, shared to WhatsApp.
  • Photo check: the app refuses photos that are too dark, blurred, flat or cut off, so every picture is usable.
  • Care reminders: due dates for vaccinations and feeding.

Status: in development. Screenshots will be added as each screen is built.

The technology behind the app

EasyFarm is an Android app built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. It stores the herd on the phone in a Room database.
