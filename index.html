Why Easy Farm?

There are a lot of challenges to farming and describing a lost animal shouldn't be one of those. The core of our app is an image and eartag library of all you livestock making it easy to control and to share information about missing animals. With Easy Farm, you can easily record and access important information about each animal, including its health, breeding history, and more.

What Easy Farm Offers

  • Scan the app opens on the camera, ready to scan an eartag or capture an image of your animal
  • Herd a database of every animal, with its photo, ear tag, species and status.
  • Roll Call keep track of all your animals during daily rounds.
  • Missing Poster create and share posters for lost or found animals.
  • Photo Check analyze the quality of images and ensures quality images are uploaded to the database.
  • Care Reminder set alerts for vaccinations, treatments, and other care needs.

Note: Easy Farm is currently in development and some features may not be fully functional yet.

The technology behind the app

Easy Farm is built using modern web technologies, and a backend framework for data management.

Progress

The development of Easy Farm is ongoing, with regular updates and improvements being made based on user feedback.
